# Getting Started

Set up your account and start issuing certificates in minutes.

## 1. Create your account

1. Visit the Sigalmark platform and click **Create an account**.
2. Enter your email address, choose a password, and optionally add your name.
3. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to confirm your account.

::: warning Note
You must verify your email address before you can log in.
:::

## 2. Join an organisation

Sigalmark is multi-tenant — each organisation operates in its own workspace. To join one you need an invitation from an Admin of that organisation.

1. Ask your organisation's Admin to send you an invitation (from **Manage → Members** in their workspace).
2. You will receive an email with a link to accept the invitation.
3. Click the link, log in (or register first), and you'll be added to the organisation.

## 3. Log in

1. Go to the login page and enter your email and password. Tick **Keep me signed in** to stay logged in for 30 days.
2. If your account has two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
3. You land on the **Certificates** page of your workspace.

## 4. Find your way around

The left sidebar is the map of the whole app:

- **Workspace → Overview** — your workspace dashboard.
- **Certificates** — the main hub, with four sections: **All** (every issued certificate), **Issue** (single and batch issuance), **Analytics** (metrics and export), and **Verify** (the public verification tool).
- **Manage \<organisation\>** — branding, members, subscription, and billing (Admins only).
- **Platform** — tenant and platform management (Operators only).

At the bottom of the sidebar you'll find the **tenant switcher** (if you belong to several organisations), your profile with your role badge, and **Sign out**.

::: tip Appearance
The round button above the tenant switcher fans out into a radial **theme switcher** — choose between the default olive theme, dark, and light.
:::

## 5. Enable two-factor authentication

For added security we recommend enabling 2FA. Go to your **Profile** page and follow the setup wizard to link an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy).

## 6. Organisation settings (Admin)

If you are an Admin, configure your organisation under **Manage \<organisation\>**:

- **Branding** — upload your logo, set QR tint colours, and pick a verification page template with live preview.
- **Members** — invite team members and assign roles (Member or Admin).
- **Subscription & Billing** — view usage meters for seats and certificates, purchase add-ons, and manage invoices.
- **Email templates** — customise the emails your recipients receive.
