## What is Sigalmark?

Sigalmark is a multi-tenant, blockchain-based digital certificate platform. Organisations use Sigalmark to issue tamper-proof certificates as ERC-721 NFTs. Each certificate gets a branded hexagonal QR code and a public verification page, so anyone can check its authenticity in seconds — no account needed.

### Key features

- **Blockchain-backed certificates** — minted as NFTs, tamper-proof and independently verifiable years later.
- **Branded certificate composites** — every certificate has a downloadable composite (your logo + its QR code) ready for email signatures and websites.
- **Multi-tenant workspaces** — each organisation gets its own workspace with members, branding, billing, and per-tenant email templates.
- **Batch issuance** — issue up to 25 certificates at a time via CSV upload, with per-row validation and error reports.
- **Email delivery** — send certificates to recipients in one click, with automated expiry reminders.
- **Analytics** — issuance metrics with filters and CSV export.
- **Mobile verification** — verification officers can scan and verify certificates on the go with the Sigalmark mobile app.

### User roles

| Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Standard access — view certificates and stats. |
| **Admin** | Manage certificates, members, branding, and billing for the organisation. |
| **Operator** | Platform-wide admin — manage tenants, subscription plans, master wallets, shortener domains, and contracts. |

Your role can differ per organisation; the badge next to your name in the sidebar shows your role in the current workspace.
