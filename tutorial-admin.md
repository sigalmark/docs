# Admin Tutorial

Everything an organisation Admin manages: the tenant dashboard, members, invitations, billing, subscriptions, and branding. Admin features live under **Manage \<organisation\>** in the sidebar.

## 1. Tenant Dashboard {#tenant-dashboard}

**Manage → Overview** summarises your organisation: certificate usage, seats, subscription state, and quick links into the sections below.

![Tenant dashboard](/images/admin-tenant-dashboard.png)

## 2. Member Management {#member-management}

**Manage → Members** lists everyone in your organisation with their role.

- Roles: **Member** (view certificates and stats) and **Admin** (everything in this tutorial).
- Change a member's role or remove them from the row actions.

![Members list](/images/admin-users-list.png)

## 3. Invitations {#invitations}

### 3.1 Send a new invitation

1. On **Manage → Members**, click **Invite**.
2. Enter the invitee's email and pick their role (Member or Admin).
3. Send — they receive an email link to accept.

![Invite a member](/images/admin-users-invite.png)

### 3.2 View pending invitations

Pending invitations are listed alongside members, marked until accepted.

### 3.3 Resend or cancel an invitation

Use the row actions on a pending invitation to resend the email or cancel it.

### 3.4 Invitation lifecycle

Sent → accepted (invitee becomes a member with the chosen role) or cancelled/expired. Invitations grant a role **in your organisation only**.

## 4. Billing & Payments {#billing}

**Manage → Billing** shows your payment method, invoices, and billing history.

![Billing summary](/images/admin-billing-summary.png)

## 5. Subscription Management {#subscription-management}

### 5.1 View current plan

**Manage → Subscription** shows your plan and two usage meters — **seats** and **certificates** — each with the included allowance, current usage, and the rate anything beyond it is billed at.

![Subscription and usage](/images/admin-subscription.png)

### 5.2 Purchase additional seats

From the Subscription page, add seats — priced at your plan's per-seat rate.

### 5.3 Purchase certificate top-ups

Add certificate allowance the same way when you're approaching the included amount.

### 5.4 Renew an expired subscription

An expired subscription blocks certificate issuance (attempts show the reason). Renew from the Subscription page; issuance resumes immediately.

## 6. Branding & Customisation {#branding}

**Manage → Branding** controls how your certificates and verification pages look. Changes preview live before you save.

### 6.1 Upload organisation logo

Upload your logo; the previews show it on light and dark surfaces. It appears in the app sidebar, on certificate composites, and on verification pages.

### 6.2 Set QR code tint colours

Choose the tint colours of your certificates' hexagonal QR codes.

### 6.3 Select verification page template

Pick one of the five templates (including Diploma Elegant and Diploma Wide) for your public verification pages.

![Branding page](/images/admin-branding.png)

### 6.4 Email templates

**Manage → Email templates** customises the six emails your recipients receive (issuance, expiry reminders, invitations, …) with your organisation's wording.

## 7. Certificate Management

Admins have every certificate capability described in the [User tutorial](/tutorial-user.html): issuing (single and batch), emailing, revoking (with confirmation), extending expiry, and the three downloads (certificate composite, QR code, artwork).
